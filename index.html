Research

My research develops statistical methodology for structured and heterogeneous data. My current interests include spatial statistics and spatial transcriptomics, survival and censored data, quantile regression, and transfer learning.

Spatial Statistics & Spatial Transcriptomics

Methods for spatially dependent data, spatial quantile regression, distributed inference, and 3-D alignment of tumor tissue sections using tumor-specific total mRNA expression.

Survival & Censored Data

Methods for censored and time-to-event outcomes, including survival-based clustering, transfer learning, and censored quantile regression.

Transfer Learning

Methods for borrowing information across related datasets or populations while accounting for heterogeneity and potential negative transfer.
