Research

Methods for heterogeneous, spatial, and censored data

My work centers on statistical methods that remain useful when data are large, dependent, heterogeneous, censored, or collected across imperfectly aligned study populations, including spatial transcriptomics and environmental health studies.

Quantile, Spatial, and Transcriptomic Inference

Smoothed and distributed quantile regression methods for spatially dependent data, including emerging problems in spatial transcriptomics.

Censored and Survival Data

Quantile regression tools for censored outcomes and survival settings, including bent-line structures and spatial survival questions.

Transfer Learning in Applications

Statistical learning for health, environmental, and drinking-water risk studies where information must be shared across related but distinct sources.
